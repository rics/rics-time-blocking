A camada de tempo para suas tarefas

Suas tarefas já existem. Aqui, elas ganham tempo.

O Rics Time-blocking não substitui seu gerenciador de tarefas. Ele traz os cards abertos do Fizzy e do Trello para você decidir quando trabalhar em cada um usando o método de time blocking.

Get started

The time layer for your tasks

Your tasks already exist. Here, they get time.

Rics Time-blocking does not replace your task manager. It brings open Fizzy and Trello cards into a calendar so you can decide when to work on each one using the time-blocking method.

Not another task manager

Work starts there. Time gets organized here.

Keep using tools built to capture, discuss, and manage work. Rics fills the missing layer: turning those tasks into real commitments on a calendar.

01

Fizzy and Trello remain the source of work.

Sync open cards and organize them on the calendar without copying all the structure, conversations, and workflows your task managers already handle.

local
02

The focus is when to work, not where to register it.

Drag a task into the calendar, adjust its duration, and spread the work across as many blocks as needed. That is time blocking in practice.

03

A local task is an exception, not the destination.

A meeting appeared without a card? Something unexpected needs time today? Add only a title inside Rics and close that gap quickly.

04

A task does not disappear when scheduled.

The same card can occupy several blocks. The list remains available to split, repeat, and reorganize work over time.

05

Local-first keeps the app free.

The browser runs the app and stores its data. With no backend, accounts, or hosted database to pay for, the project can be offered without a fee.
